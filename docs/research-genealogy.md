# DIO/ODI Research Genealogy

## Interpretive Genealogy

The [Genealogical Foundations of DIO/ODI](https://vocab.danielberanek.cz/research-genealogy/foundations/) is the interpretive narrative for the historical, professional, intellectual, and methodological lines that converged in DIO/ODI. It explains how the recurring principles emerged across different contexts.

This registry remains the evidential and provenance layer. It records research objects, source records, exploratory processes, publication artefacts, archival observations, and cross-cutting threads. The narrative page does not replace the registry or duplicate its record-level provenance.

The connection is reciprocal at record level: relevant research-object and intellectual-thread records carry an optional `interpretive_refs` array. The registry frontend renders these as links to the exact section anchors in Foundations. These are navigation references to the interpretive account, not additional provenance relations or claims that the linked source records are identical.

The Research Genealogy Registry records the source object, exploratory process, dated concept-development milestones, and intermediate artefacts, alternative publication manifestations, and archive observations associated with the emergence of Digital Identity Optimization (DIO) and the Ontology of Digital Identity (ODI).

It is a research-process layer within the Reference Model's operational metadata. It is maintained separately from the publication registry, distribution registry, canonical Reference Vocabulary, and Semantic Change Feed.

## Research object

The primary object is the living, author-maintained professional ethnography:

- [Od pultu přes ZUR a PSY až k SEO a copy s ostrými hroty! AI a dál…](https://danielberanek.cz/od-pultu-az-k-seo-a-copy-s-ostrymi-hroty-profesni-bio/)

The object is multimodal and layered. It combines a non-linear professional narrative with interpretive notes, a visual semantic map, a semantic inventory, linked source material, machine-readable instructions and metadata, and an author-maintained changelog. These components together formed the material examined during the exploratory process.

## Exploratory process

The registry describes the process as **AI-assisted secondary exploration of a multimodal professional-ethnography object**.

AI systems produced successive readings and interpretations of the source material. The author examined these readings against the source text and its context. Cross-domain associations, implicit readings, interpretive discrepancies, and algorithmic noise became material for further inquiry and conceptual synthesis.

This was an iterative exploratory process rather than a pre-specified linear sequence. The professional ethnography's changelog records the key transition on 21 May 2026: the broad interdisciplinary text had organically developed into a new disciplinary concept; DIO was given its own place in the structure, work on its Manifest began, and the ethnography was explicitly described as the empirical and asynchronous foundation from which DIO was derived. On 22 June 2026, ODI was articulated as the broader theoretical layer behind DIO.

## Recurring intellectual lineages and earlier research works

The professional ethnography describes semiotics, communication theory, structuralism, poststructuralism, and discourse theory as recurring intellectual foundations across the author's academic work. They should therefore be read as **cross-cutting theoretical lines**, not as isolated concepts assigned to only one thesis. Their expression changes with the research object:

- [Reartikulace konceptů ideologie a hegemonie v rámci mediálních studií](https://is.muni.cz/th/ajfb6/) (2011) works explicitly with ideology, hegemony, discourse, nodal points, and poststructuralist rearticulation.
- [Sémiotická analýza klipu Marilyna Mansona mOBSCENE](https://danielberanek.cz/reference/akademicke-publikace/semioticka-analyza-klipu-marilyna-mansona-mobscene/) (2005) uses semiotic deconstruction and reconstruction of signification in an audiovisual text.
- [Klíčové aspekty sebevražedných sekt z hlediska jedince](https://is.muni.cz/th/d2622/) (2006; [author-supplied IS MU route](https://is.muni.cz/th/78577/fss_b/)) combines psychological concepts with structuralist semiotics, memetics, ideology, propaganda, and the synthesis of a model of the power situation. The professional ethnography explicitly carries that model into its reflection on commercial conversion, identity, needs, vulnerabilities, and brand communication.
- The 2009 positive-psychology thesis lineage combines a distinctive methodological strand with that wider theoretical background.

### Positive psychology: archived files and two examination attempts

The IS MU record [wknmn](https://is.muni.cz/th/wknmn/) contains several separately stored files from different dates. They must not be conflated as one PDF or treated as independent research works.

- **20 May 2008:** an earlier file named [Pozitivni_psychologie_a_Castanedovo_uceni.doc](https://is.muni.cz/th/wknmn/Pozitivni_psychologie_a_Castanedovo_uceni.doc), with a corresponding [Archive.pdf](https://is.muni.cz/th/wknmn/Pozitivni_psychologie_a_Castanedovo_uceni_Archive.pdf?kodomez=termin-540379;lang=en). This is the file linked in the original source discussion. Its content relationship to the later January 2009 main text has not yet been established by direct comparison.
- **5 January 2009:** the first-attempt main body file [Kvalitativni_evaluace_pozitivni_PSY_perspektivou_Castanedova_uceni.doc](https://is.muni.cz/th/wknmn/Kvalitativni_evaluace_pozitivni_PSY_perspektivou_Castanedova_uceni.doc), accompanied by a separately stored [opening-pages-and-contents file](https://is.muni.cz/th/wknmn/uvodni_strany_a_obsah.doc). The author-supplied 88-page DOC reviewed for this registry is the main body file. It contains chapters 1–10, including the conclusion and sources, but begins directly with chapter 1 because the opening pages and table of contents are stored separately.
- The first examination attempt on **20 January 2009** was not defended. The IS MU catalogue record uses the title *Pozitivní psychologie a vybrané duchovní směry: komparativní analýza*, while the January body file identifies its text as *Kvalitativní evaluace pozitivní psychologie perspektivou Castanedova učení*.
- **29 May 2009:** the same first-version archive lists another separately stored file, [uvodni_strany.doc](https://is.muni.cz/th/wknmn/uvodni_strany.doc). The author confirms that this file also belongs to the **first version**; it must not be assigned to the second attempt.
- The second attempt, *Aktualizace východisek a možností pozitivní psychologie*, is held in a **different IS MU archive**, [record k2bkc](https://is.muni.cz/th/k2bkc/), as one complete thesis file. It was successfully defended on **2 June 2009**. Its [ResearchGate full text and DOI](https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.32958.86087) identify this successful second version.

The IS MU archive lists DOC, PDF, Archive.pdf, and TXT renditions for individual attachments. The Archive.pdf suffix therefore identifies an archived representation of a particular file, not necessarily a merged PDF containing the title pages, contents, and body held in separate attachments. The available file inventory does not identify who produced a particular PDF conversion or establish that its defects originated in the authoring DOC. Do not call the 2008 Archive.pdf the complete first-attempt thesis until it has been compared with the other stored files.

The two submissions form one thesis-development lineage with two distinct examination states: the January 2009 first attempt was not defended; the substantially revised second submission was successfully defended on 2 June 2009. The second version's own section 2.8 documents the revision. It sharpens the methodological definitions and research plan, revises data processing, and adds a separately articulated secondary theoretical foundation. It adds a worked analysis of Love's text in chapter 6.1, presents the agreements/conflicts/differences comparison in tabular form with a supplement in chapter 7, completely rewrites the section on possibilities for extending positive psychology in chapter 8, and revises the conclusion and sources. The author reports 29% revised content, 173 to 257 cited bibliography items, and 182 to 573 footnotes; the reported similarity to the earlier version is 71%.

The methodological contribution is Bateson's binocular-vision principle: select analogous but differently coded perspectives, reconstruct and reproduce both, compare and interpret them, then extrapolate new possibilities. The second version explicitly names semiotics and signification (including Saussure and Barthes), Derrida's différance, ethnomethodology, and critical discourse analysis as part of its secondary theoretical background. The professional ethnography generalizes the binocular-perspective method into continuing practice: compare human-language effectiveness with search/AI algorithms, and business aims with people's actual needs and emotions.

## Outputs, manifestations, and archive observations

The registry distinguishes three layers that may otherwise be counted as separate research outputs:

- **Underlying research output:** an analysis, synthesis, interpretation, or other substantive work.
- **Publication manifestation:** a particular web page, shared AI conversation, Canvas artifact, deployed application, PDF, or SlideShare presentation through which that work is presented. A manifestation may be linked to an existing output when the relationship is supported; a candidate relationship remains labelled as such until the content is compared.
- **Archive observation:** a time-stamped capture of a manifestation in Wayback Machine, Megalodon, or Archive.today / Archive.is / Archive.ph. Multiple captures of one manifestation do not create additional research outputs.

Archive timestamps are preserved in UTC. For Megalodon URLs, the source timestamp and Asia/Tokyo (JST) basis are also recorded before normalization. A timestamp identifies an archive observation, not necessarily the creation, completion, or first-publication date of the underlying work. Capture content remains marked unreviewed until inspected.

Where the author supplied an original platform URL but the artifact's content or model version has not been inspected, the record keeps a provisional title and a content-review status. Similar titles or common topics do not by themselves establish identity between two outputs.
