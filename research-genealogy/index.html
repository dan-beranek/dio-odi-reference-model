DIO/ODI Research Genealogy

A navigable record of the research objects, exploratory processes, intermediate outputs, publication forms, and archival traces associated with the development of Digital Identity Optimization (DIO) and the Ontology of Digital Identity (ODI).

The registry distinguishes an underlying research output from its web, chat, application, document, or presentation manifestations—and from later archive captures of those manifestations. Similar topics do not automatically establish that two records are the same output.

Genealogical Foundations of DIO/ODI provides the interpretive narrative connecting the historical, intellectual, professional, and methodological roots documented by this registry. Relevant object and intellectual-thread cards link back to the specific Foundations sections in which they are interpreted.

Research objects

The sources and subjects examined or used as professional and research context.

Research processes

Processes are reconstructed from available source records and author-provided context. Their bounds are not inferred from archive timestamps alone.

Thematic clusters

Clusters group related records without collapsing distinct outputs or asserting unverified content identity.

Recurring intellectual and professional-practice threads

Cross-cutting lines connect research objects without collapsing distinct works or treating every expression as the same output.

Timeline and archive observations

The timeline combines dated research milestones, dated source observations, and archive captures. Archive dates indicate when a representation was captured, not when its underlying output was created.

Artefact register

Each record preserves its own platform identity. Additional manifestations and archive captures are nested under the underlying record where their relationship is known or explicitly marked as a candidate.

These archive references are retained without forcing an unsupported connection to an artefact.
